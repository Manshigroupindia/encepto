## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies the discipline of deep-tech artificial intelligence research and frontier physical engineering: precise, structural, understated, and intellectually rigorous. It rejects the ephemeral cliches of retail AI—eschewing neon purples, glowing orbs, and vaporous gradients in favor of high-craft architectural geometry, authoritative contrast, and editorial typography.

The aesthetic fuses German industrial functionalism (typified by Braun and Otl Aicher) with contemporary editorial layout and technical instrument panels. Visual density is strictly calibrated: generous whitespace frames intricate technical telemetry, code blocks, and model convergence plots. The tone is calm, uncompromising, and deeply confident—designed to signal profound scientific credibility to enterprise partners, research scientists, and sovereign infrastructure institutions.

## Colors

The system relies on a high-craft physical paper and graphite metaphor for its primary light mode, shifting to an instrument-grade slate dark mode for terminal and monitoring environments.

### Light Mode (Primary Default)
- **Base Canvas (`#FBF9F5`):** Warm ivory representing physical archival stock.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F3EFEA`):** Soft structural beige for nested modules, inspector sidebars, and input wells.
- **Primary Ink (`#1A1918`):** Deep charcoal for primary text, structural grid dividers, and prominent geometric glyphs.
- **Deep Graphite (`#111110`):** Solid foundation tone used for high-emphasis callouts, primary buttons, and heavy horizontal rules.
- **Muted Structural (`#787571`):** Muted grey for auxiliary metadata, timestamps, units of measure, and axis lines.
- **Hairline Division (`#E5E0D8`):** Crisp boundary lines establishing strict geometric enclosure.
- **Technical Cyan Accent (`#0284C7` / `#0EA5E9`):** Precision instrument indicator reserved exclusively for active states, data stream status, convergence marks, and primary focus pins.

### Dark Mode (Operational Environment)
- **Base Canvas (`#0D0E11`):** Deep charcoal substrate.
- **Surface Elevation (`#16181D`):** Slate graphite tier.
- **Primary Ink (`#F5F5F7`):** Warm optical white for crisp reading contrast without glare.
- **Muted Metadata (`#9CA3AF`):** Medium grey for passive telemetry.
- **Boundary Hairline (`#272B33`):** Low-contrast structural perimeter.
- **Active Telemetry (`#0EA5E9`):** High-visibility technical cyan.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the architectural clarity of Geist with the computational rigor of Space Mono.

- **Geist (Headings & Display):** Serves as the structural framework for editorial titling, structural section anchors, and thematic announcements. Set tight with negative letter-spacing to evoke precision mechanical type.
- **Space Mono (Body, Telemetry, and Metadata):** Forms the functional core of the reading experience. Monospaced body copy conveys engineering utility, treats data outputs as first-class content, and aligns tabular data natively.
- **Case Conventions:** All component labels, structural breadcrumbs, section markers, and field tags are strictly uppercase (`mono-label`) with open tracking (`0.06em`). Running prose remains standard sentence case.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a modular, rigid 12-column architectural grid with hairline partition borders. Rather than soft floating elements, layout boundaries are rendered explicitly via borders that evoke technical schematics and blueprints.

### Grid Rhythm & Reflow
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid, 3rem margins, 1.5rem gutters. Layouts are partitioned with structural hairlines (`#E5E0D8` in light mode, `#272B33` in dark mode).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid, 2rem margins, 1rem gutters. Supplementary inspectors fold beneath primary consoles.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column single-axis stack, 1rem margins, 0.75rem gutters. Complex multi-axis telemetry switches to horizontally scrolling data tapes.

### Spacing Philosophy
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px): Micro-alignment of data chips, icon-to-metric offsets, and status pips.
- `space-md` (16px) and `space-lg` (24px): Standard internal padding for cards, code panels, and form controls.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section breaks between architectural modules and analytical readouts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is conveyed entirely without diffuse shadows. The design system uses strict **planar layering and hairline boundaries** to structure space:

1. **Substrate Base (Level 0):** Pure `#FBF9F5` canvas representing the base environment.
2. **Contained Surface (Level 1):** Solid `#F3EFEA` background fills enclosed by a 1px solid border (`#E5E0D8`). Used for data panels, telemetry readouts, and cards.
3. **Interactive & Active Overlays (Level 2):** Modals, contextual command palettes, and floating tooltips employ solid opaque backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` in light mode, `#16181D` in dark mode) bordered by high-contrast hairlines (`#1A1918` or `#0EA5E9`).
4. **Mechanical Accents:** Depth interactions are signaled via hairline color shifts and inverted tonal blocks (e.g., hover states invert from beige background with dark text to solid `#1A1918` background with `#FBF9F5` text). No blur or diffusion filters are permitted.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). Every element—from primary call-to-actions, badges, code containers, to modal overlays—terminates at acute 90-degree angles with zero border-radius.

This uncompromising rectilinear geometry reinforces structural permanence, industrial equipment aesthetics, and engineering precision. When visual differentiation is needed, elements employ technical clipped corners (45-degree 4px chamfers) via CSS clipping paths, or inset geometric notches, rather than rounded curves.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1A1918` background, `#FBF9F5` text, zero border-radius. Font: `Space Mono` uppercase, 11px, bold. Hover transitions to `#111110` with an active 1px solid `#0284C7` right-hand status indicator.
- **Secondary / Technical Outlined:** `#FBF9F5` background, 1px solid `#1A1918` border, `#1A1918` text. Hover state inverts completely (background becomes `#1A1918`, text becomes `#FBF9F5`).
- **Telemetry Ghost:** Transparent background with hairline `#E5E0D8` perimeter, `#787571` text. On hover, border and text snap to `#0284C7`.

### Data Chips & Badges
- Strict rectangular tags with 1px solid `#E5E0D8` borders, `#F3EFEA` fill, and 11px uppercase `Space Mono` text.
- Status badges feature a 4x4px square indicator pip: green `#10B981` (nominal), amber `#F59E0B` (evaluating), cyan `#0284C7` (training/streaming), or red `#EF4444` (halted).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background: `#F3EFEA` (light) / `#16181D` (dark).
- Border: 1px solid `#E5E0D8`. Focus state shifts the border to 1px solid `#1A1918` with a 2px vertical cyan `#0284C7` marker on the left edge.
- Typography: `Space Mono`, 13px. Labels are positioned above the field in uppercase 11px `mono-label`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox:** 14x14px sharp square with 1px solid `#1A1918` border. Checked state fills solid `#1A1918` with an interior 6x6px sharp square cutout in ivory `#FBF9F5`.
- **Radio:** 14x14px sharp square rotated 45 degrees (diamond geometry) to eliminate circular forms while preserving standard radio exclusivity conventions.

### Structural Cards & Panels
- Constructed with `#F3EFEA` surface fill and 1px solid `#E5E0D8` frame.
- Headers are separated by a continuous horizontal 1px hairline rule and display uppercase metadata tags on the left, paired with coordinate or version markers (e.g., `LOC // 42.3601° N`) on the right.

### Telemetry Stream & Log Tables
- Fixed-width column alignments. Alternating rows use hairline divider borders rather than alternating background stripes. Monospaced numeric data displays right-aligned with fixed tabular figures. Active records are highlighted with a 2px left border in `#0284C7`.
